Hi, I’m Jianing 👋
PhD candidate working at the intersection of MRI imaging, neurovascular science, and computation methods.

I build imaging methods to measure things that are small, fast, and annoyingly difficult to see.

🔬 What am I curious about?

Tiny vessels
How do blood vessels smaller than a millimeter tell us about brain health?

Things that move
How do blood and CSF dynamics change with aging and neurodegenerative disease?


🧠 Things I’m working on

Seeing the brain’s tiny arteries
Measuring cerebral perforating arteries with 2D submillimeter dualVENC phase-contrast MRI.

When blood meets CSF
Understanding how vascular pulsatility couples with cerebrospinal fluid motion.

CSF dynamics in subarachnoid space
Developing MRI imaging sequence based on GRASE to capture CSF dynamics in the subarachnoid space.

🌱 Things I hope to explore
New ways to acquire and reconstruct MRI
I’m excited to explore non-Cartesian trajectories and advanced reconstruction methods to push the limits of spatial and temporal resolution.

Deep learning for quantitative imaging
I hope to explore how deep learning can improve MRI reconstruction and help uncover subtle physiological signatures of disease.
